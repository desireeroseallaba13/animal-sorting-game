🐣 ANIMAL SORTING CHALLENGE 🐄

Use your hand to sort the animals — no mouse needed!

⭐ Score: 0/10
⏱️ Time: 60s
🖐️ Gesture: Waiting…
HOW TO PLAY
1. Click Start Camera and allow camera access.
2. Show ☝️ one index finger and move the orange cursor over an animal card.
3. Show 🖐️ an open palm to pick up that animal.
4. Point to a category, then make ✊ a fist to drop it.
5. Explain why the animal belongs there to earn the point!
Tip: Keep your hand visible, well-lit, and a little away from the camera.
Press “Start Camera” to begin.
